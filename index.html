WHO WE ARE

A movement, not just a party

BalkanMadness was born from a simple idea: the Netherlands deserved a home for Balkan music's biggest names. We book the artists, fill the rooms, and turn every event into a night people talk about for months. From intimate concerts to full-blown parties, we bring the energy of the Balkans to Dutch soil — loud, proud, and unforgettable.
